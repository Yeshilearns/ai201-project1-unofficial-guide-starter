Queens College AI Skill Foundry – 3-Hour Micro-Internship

Source:
https://aiskillfoundry.com/3hmi-application

The AI Skill Foundry 3-Hour Micro-Internship provides students with an opportunity to work on real business challenges using generative AI.

Program Overview

- Complete a short application.
- Finish a 10-day Responsible AI course.
- Complete an assessment.
- Eligible students receive an invitation to a live 3-hour micro-internship session.

During the Micro-Internship

Students:
- Solve real business problems.
- Apply responsible AI practices.
- Create client-ready deliverables.
- Receive staff feedback.

Outcomes

Participants may receive:
- Portfolio-ready project
- Practical AI experience
- AI competency badge
- Performance-based stipend

The program is sponsored by the CUNY Office of Academic Affairs AI Innovation Fund and supported by the Tech Incubator at Queens College.
