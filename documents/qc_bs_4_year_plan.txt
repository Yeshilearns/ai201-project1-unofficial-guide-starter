Queens College Computer Science BS 4-Year Plan

soruce: https://www.qc.cuny.edu/aac/wp-content/uploads/sites/84/2022/12/Computer-Science-BS-4-Year-Plan.pdf


This document provides a recommended four-year academic plan for students pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Queens College.

The plan outlines:

* Recommended semester-by-semester course sequence
* General Education requirements
* Mathematics requirements
* Core Computer Science courses
* Science requirements
* Free electives
