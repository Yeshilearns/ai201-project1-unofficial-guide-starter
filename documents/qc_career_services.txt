Queens College Center for Career Engagement and Internships (CEI)

Source:
https://www.qc.cuny.edu/academics/cei/

The Center for Career Engagement and Internships (CEI) helps Queens College students prepare for their careers by providing coaching, internships, job opportunities, and professional connections.

Services

- One-on-one career advising
- Internship and job search assistance
- Resume and cover letter reviews
- Mock interviews
- Career workshops
- Career and internship fairs
- Networking opportunities
- Career assessments through FOCUS 2
- Professional development resources

Career Resources

- Handshake for internship and job postings
- Career maps by major
- Resume and cover letter templates
- Vault Career Intelligence
- Graduate school resources
- Internship Stipend Fund
- Career readiness workshops

Students can schedule appointments with career educators for personalized career guidance.
