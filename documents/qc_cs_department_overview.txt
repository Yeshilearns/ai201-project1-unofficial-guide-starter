Queens College Computer Science Department Overview

Source:
https://www.cs.qc.cuny.edu/

The Queens College Department of Computer Science prepares students for careers in computing through undergraduate and graduate education, research opportunities, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

The department offers:

* Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in Computer Science
* Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) in Computer Science
* Master of Arts (M.A.) in Computer Science
* Doctoral research opportunities through the CUNY Graduate Center
* Undergraduate minors in Computer Science, Computer Information Technology (CIT), Computational Linguistics, and Financial Modeling

Department Mission

* Offer undergraduate and graduate academic programs with rigorous curricula that develop a strong understanding of computer science principles and practical technical skills.
* Conduct research in computer science and interdisciplinary computational fields with opportunities for undergraduate, master's, and doctoral student participation.
* Promote computation as a tool for scientific discovery and collaboration across departments and the CUNY Graduate Center.
* Provide computing and information technology education for Queens College students through service courses and interdisciplinary academic programs.

Academic Opportunities

Students can pursue both Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Science degrees in Computer Science. The B.S. program includes additional mathematics, laboratory, and advanced computer science coursework, while the B.A. provides a broader liberal arts education combined with computer science.

Research and Student Engagement

Faculty members conduct research in areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, cybersecurity, databases, distributed systems, algorithms, and data science. Students have opportunities to participate in research projects and seminars alongside faculty members.
