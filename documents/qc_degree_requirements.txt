Queens College Computer Science BS Degree Requirements

Source:
https://www.cs.qc.cuny.edu/undergrad/BS_FA22.pdf

This document outlines the curriculum and graduation requirements for the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Queens College.

The BS degree requires completion of:

- General Education (QC Core) requirements
- Mathematics requirements
- Core Computer Science courses
- Science requirements
- Computer Science electives
- Free electives

Required Mathematics Courses
- MATH 120 - Discrete Mathematics
- MATH 151 - Calculus I
- MATH 152 - Calculus II
- MATH 231 or MATH 237 - Linear Algebra
- MATH 241 -  Probability and Statistics

Required Core Computer Science Courses
- CSCI 111 - Introduction to Algorithmic Problem Solving
- CSCI 211 - Object-Oriented Programming in C++
- CSCI 212 - Object-Oriented Programming in Java
- CSCI 220 - Discrete Structures
- CSCI 240 - Computer Organization and Assembly Language
- CSCI 313 - Data Structures
- CSCI 316 - Principles of Programming Languages
- CSCI 320 - Theory of Computation
- CSCI 323 - Design and Analysis of Algorithms
- CSCI 331 - Database Systems
- CSCI 340 - Operating Systems
- CSCI 343 - Computer Architecture
- CSCI 370 - Software Engineering

Additional Requirements
- Complete the required Computer Science electives.
- Complete General Education requirements.
- Earn the minimum required grades in major courses.
